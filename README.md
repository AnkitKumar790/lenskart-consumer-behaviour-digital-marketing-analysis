# lenskart-consumer-behaviour-digital-marketing-analysis
A primary research-based analysis of Lenskart’s consumer behaviour, digital marketing, and omnichannel customer experience using survey data from 85 respondents and field observations across four retail stores.
### Consumer Behaviour & Digital Marketing Analysis of Lenskart

**Category:** Consumer Research | Digital Marketing

As part of the Corporate Mentorship Programme at FIIB, I conducted a study examining how digital touchpoints and in-store experiences influence consumer behaviour at Lenskart.

**Research approach**
- Conducted a structured survey involving 85 respondents.
- Observed customer experiences across four Lenskart retail stores in Delhi.
- Analysed lens information clarity, package explanations, digital influence, customer satisfaction and recommendation behaviour.
- Examined differences in product merchandising and customer interactions across premium and non-premium outlets.

**Key learning**

The study highlighted the importance of clear product communication, consistent in-store service and the integration of digital tools with physical retail experiences in shaping customer confidence and brand recommendation behaviour.

**Skills demonstrated:** Consumer Behaviour Analysis, Primary Market Research, Survey Analysis, Digital Marketing, Customer Insights and Omnichannel Marketing.
